HOW IT WORKS:
  Stage 1: Creates folder structure (2021_H1, 2021_H2, ... 2026_H1)
  Stage 2: Discovers & categorizes your L2 zips by date range
  Stage 3: Asks for confirmation before moving (safe!)
  Stage 4: Organizes archives into semester folders
  Stage 5: Audits parquet schema, columns, quality, bid-ask spreads
  
  Output: Audit reports in results/research_master/l2_parquet_audit/
